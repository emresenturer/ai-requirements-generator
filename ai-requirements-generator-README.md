# AI Requirements Generator

How AI can turn unstructured stakeholder meeting notes into structured product requirements documentation.

## The problem

Business analysts spend hours translating meeting discussions into structured requirements documents. The manual process is slow and inconsistent across teams.

## The solution

An AI workflow that takes raw meeting notes and produces:

- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- Business rules
- Functional and non-functional requirements
- Documentation ready for product teams

## Workflow

1. Accept raw stakeholder meeting notes
2. Identify business objectives
3. Generate structured requirements
4. Produce user stories and acceptance criteria
5. Output documentation ready for product teams

## Tools

Claude · ChatGPT · Notion · Python

## Business impact

Reduces documentation time by up to 70% and improves consistency across product teams. By [Emre Senturer](https://github.com/emresenturer).
